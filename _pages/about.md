Hi! I am a Senior Research Scientist at the Accenture Center for Advanced AI in Mountain View, California, where I joined in 2026. My research focuses on multimodal agents, multi-agent reinforcement learning, and visually grounded reasoning for long horizon tasks.


I completed my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Princeton University in 2025, advised by <a href='https://ncclab.princeton.edu/'>Jonathan D. Cohen</a> (<a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&amp;hl=en&amp;user=5V-xQYUAAAAJ&amp;citation_for_view=5V-xQYUAAAAJ:r0BpntZqJG4C' title='Integration of Object-Centric Processing and Relational Inductive Biases for Abstract Visual Reasoning'>Ph.D. thesis</a>). My doctoral research drew on cognitive science and neuroscience to develop neural networks with human-like systematic and sample-efficient generalization, with a focus on object-centric representations and abstract visual reasoning.

During the summers of 2023 and 2024, I interned at Microsoft Research, New York, with <a href='https://www.momen-nejad.org/'>Ida Momennejad</a>, developing a modular, multi-agent architecture using large language models to improve multi-step planning. This work was published in <a href='https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-63804-5'>Nature Communications in 2025</a>. At Princeton, I also worked on projects with <a href='https://pni.princeton.edu/people/h-sebastian-seung'>Sebastian Seung</a> and <a href='https://cocosci.princeton.edu/tom/tom.php'>Tom Griffiths</a>.

Before Princeton, I earned my B.Tech. at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, where I worked with <a href='http://www.facweb.iitkgp.ac.in/~debdoot/'>Debdoot Sheet</a> at the intersection of deep learning and biomedical imaging. I also interned at Adobe Research, Bangalore, with <a href='https://sites.google.com/site/subratamitraweb/'>Subrata Mitra</a>, developing a deep reinforcement learning scheduler to improve resource utilization and application performance.
